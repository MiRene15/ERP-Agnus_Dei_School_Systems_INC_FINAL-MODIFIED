## II. Institutional Policies, Rules, and Regulations

### A. Admission Policies and Requirements
**1. New Students (from Kinder to Senior HS)**
* **1.1 AGE**
  * 1.1.1. Kinder 1 - 4 years old by the opening of the school year 
  * 1.1.2. Kinder 2 - 5 years old by the opening of the school year 
  * 1.1.3. Grade 1 - 6 years old by the opening of the school year 
* **1.2 Certification:** Certification from the Kindergarten school last attended (for entering Grade 1). All other entrants to Grade 1 who came from other schools shall undergo a Read-Aloud Exercise and physical initial interview.
* **1.3 Form 138:** Certified true copy of the latest Form 138 (Report Card).
* **1.4 Birth Certificate:** PSA-authenticated (for new enrollees).
* **1.5 Certificate of Good Moral Character:** Duly signed by the Principal of the school last attended.
* **1.6 Pictures:** Two (2) 2x2 colored ID pictures with white background.
* **1.7 Interviews:** Incoming Junior High School students who came from other public/private schools and other transferees shall undergo interviews conducted by the JHS Department Coordinator and/or School Head.
* **1.8 Forms:** Accomplished Student Data Form and Parent Data Form.

**Additional Requirements for SHS Entrants**
* **1.10 JHS Graduates from ESC-Certified Private Schools:** ESC Grant Certificate.
* **1.11 JHS Graduates from Non-ESC-Certified Private Schools:** SHS Voucher.

**2. Old Students**
* **2.1** Form 138 (Report Card)

**B. Admission Procedure**
1. Fill up an application form and submit the same to the teacher-in-charge of admission, together with all other essential documents and requirements.
2. New enrollees and transferees shall be interviewed by the teacher-in-charge of admission for initial assessment. Review of grades in Form 138 shall be undertaken as well.
3. Pay the necessary fees to be officially enrolled.
4. Must attend the orientation activity before the opening of classes to become fully informed of the prevailing policies of the School.

**C. Curriculum Year Level**
Only students with no failing marks and a general average of 75% more are eligible for readmission to the next year level. In some instances, students who do not meet the required general average may be readmitted under academic probation in the school year under the following circumstances:
1. They have taken remedial classes during the preceding summer and have passed the same.
2. They do not have behavioral/disciplinary records.

### B. Academic & Grading Policies

**1. Automated Grading Components (DepEd Standard)**
Academic excellence shall be based on the general average the student got from Written Work, Performance Tasks, and Quarterly Assessment. These three areas are given specific percentage weights that vary according to the nature of the learning area.

**Table 1. Weight of the Components for Grades 1 – 10**
| Components | Languages / AP / ESP | Science / Math | MAPEH / EPP / TLE |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Written Work** | 30% | 40% | 40% |
| **Performance Task** | 50% | 40% | 40% |
| **Quarterly Assessment** | 20% | 20% | 20% |

**Table 2. Weight of the Components for SHS (Grades 11 – 12)**
| Components | Core Subjects | Academic Track: All Other Subjects | Academic Track: Work Immersion / Research | TVL / Sports / Arts & Design Track |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Written Work** | 25% | 25% | 35% | 20% |
| **Performance Task** | 50% | 45% | 40% | 60% |
| **Quarterly Assessment** | 25% | 30% | 25% | 20% |

**2. Recognition of Merit / Selection of Honor Students**
* **C.1.** Candidates for honors in all levels shall have no grade lower than 87% in any subject. The top five achievers of each curriculum level will be recognized at the end of each quarter. Rankings for the top five (5) achievers will not be announced until the last quarter of the school term. 
* **C.2.** To qualify for honors, a student must not have any major derogatory record or violate school policies.
* **C.3.** The top five (5) achievers shall be ranked using the **7-3 Point Scheme** (7 points for Academic Performance and 3 points for Co-curricular Activities).
* **C.5.** In the final ranking (Refer to DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015):
  * **98-100**: Highest Honor
  * **95-97**: High Honor
  * **90-94**: With Honor

### C. Financial Policies
It is deemed that once a student is enrolled, a commitment is made for the whole school year. 

**1. Schedule of Payments**
* **Plan A (Cash):** All fees are paid upon enrollment. A ten (10%) percent discount in tuition fees is granted.
* **Plan B (Monthly):** Upon enrollment, Php 1,500 + registration and miscellaneous fees. The balance is divided into months and must be fully settled before the end of the school year.
* **Plan C (Alternative Plan):** Crafted via a meeting with the parent, cashier, and registrar due to unforeseeable circumstances affecting regular payment.

**2. Withdrawal and Refunds**
A student withdrawing after the fourth week of classes will be charged the tuition fees for the entire school year. No official clearance will be released unless accounts are paid. Refunds are based on:
* Before the first week of classes: 100%
* After a week of classes: 90%
* After the second week of classes: 80% 
* After the third week of classes: 70%
* After the fourth week of classes: 0% *(There are no refunds under Plan B).*

> **System note (2026-08-06):** The live `payment_plan` field is a VARCHAR with values `installment|full` (the "Plan A/B/C" labels above are policy documentation, not DB enums). Plan A cash = `full`, Plan B monthly = `installment`; Plan C is handled manually via cashier/registrar agreement.

**3. Scholarships and Privileges & Discount**
* **Honors Program:** Grade 6 Rank 1 receives **100% discount**, Rank 2 receives **50% discount**, Rank 3 receives **25% discount**. JHS Students (Grades 7 to 10): Rank 1 (**100%**), Rank 2 (**75%**), Rank 3 (**50%**).
* **Family Discount:** Enrolling multiple children grants a 10% discount to the second child, and a 15% discount to the third child.
* **ESC Grant:** Php 9,000 for qualifying Grade 7 students, released at the latter part of the term (not applicable at enrollment).

## III. System Architecture & Security Standards

To ensure the system is secure and compliant with institutional rules and the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**, the following technical constraints are enforced:

* **Scope Control (Offline Ledger):** The ERP restricts payment processing exclusively to physical, face-to-face transactions per the Financial Policies. The system securely acts as a digital ledger.
* **Unified Account Proxy:** Students and Parents share a single, unified Portal Account to simplify access and track the monthly financial statements (Plan B).
* **Password Hashing:** All system passwords are computationally hashed using Laravel’s Bcrypt algorithm.
* **Middleware Routing:** Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensures isolated dashboard views.
* **Data Archiving:** Sensitive demographics and medical logs are securely retained rather than permanently deleted, fulfilling legal data retention policies.

## IV. Core Operational Workflow (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Pre-Admission & Inquiry
1. **Public Engagement:** Prospective students explore programs, admission policies, and view **Public Announcements** published by the Admin.
2. **Student Number Generation:** Upon identity verification, the applicant receives a strict Institutional Student Number (`[EnrollmentYear]-[UniqueID]`, e.g., `2026-00123`). 

### Phase 2: Application Phase
3. **Admission Application:** The applicant completes their primary profile, fulfilling all age requirements (e.g., K1 = 4 years old).
4. **Requirements Upload:** Secure digital uploading of Birth Certificates, Form 138, and Good Moral profiles.
5. **Registrar Approval:** The Registrar verifies submitted documents. Automated email notifications clear the student for the Finance gate.

### Phase 3: Financial & Enrollment Phase
6. **Tuition Assessment:** The system computes the grade-level fee, prompting the user for **Plan A, Plan B, or Plan C**, and automatically applying honors or family discounts.
7. **Payment Processing:** The Cashier logs the physical payment. This triggers an automated digital receipt and flags the student as "Financially Cleared."
8. **Enrollment & Sectioning:** The Registrar officially categorizes the student: assigning sections and required curriculum subjects.

### Phase 4: Active Student Ecosystem
9. **Portal Activation:** The unified Student/Parent account unlocks to view Class Schedules, monitor pending balances, and track academic grades.

### Phase 5: End of Term & Progression
10. **Promotion Processing:** Upon checking constraints (no failing marks, >75% GWA), students are staged for promotion. The **7-3 Point Scheme** evaluates the top achievers.
11. **Graduation Clearance:** Automated clearance checks against the Cashier and Librarian ledgers ensure no "Back Accounts" exist.

## V. Cross-Functional Process Flows

* **Old Student Registration:** Returning students bypass document uploads. They input their legacy LRN, update contact info, and await evaluation for legacy holds before proceeding to Payment.
* **Cashier Process:** Applies Plan A/B/C payment terms, evaluates manual discounts, inputs over-the-counter payments and automatically blocks non-paying students (Back Accounts) from taking the Monthly/Periodical tests.
* **Teacher Process:** Teachers instruct students and log PTC compliance. When grading opens, Teachers input raw scores based on four assessment types (**Written Work, Quiz, Seatwork, Exam**). Grade finalization is blocked for students flagged as non-PTC compliant or those holding Back Accounts.
* **Librarian Process:** The Librarian **manually inputs** a student's institutional Student Number to log their daily facility usage and to trace borrowed books.
* **School Nurse Process:** Access demographic profiles to log clinic incident dates, symptoms, and provided treatments.
* **System Admin Process:** Creates users, enforces access roles, captures audit trails, and publishes **Announcements.**

* **Offline Payment Terminal:** Log physical cash payments directly against a student's profile to issue digital receipts under the Plan A/B/C terms.
* **Organizational Ledger:** Track aggregate pending balances and flag students with "Back Accounts" to block examinations.

* **Workload & Roster View:** Instantly access active Class Lists explicitly defined by the Registrar.

### 7. School Nurse Dashboard

### 8. Directress Dashboard
* **Fee Schedule Management:** Full CRUD for grade-level, term, and school-year fee configurations (Kinder through Grade 12, 3 terms per school year).
* **Graduation Fee Management:** Full CRUD for Grade 6, 10, and 12 graduation fees with student assignment and paid status toggling.

### 9. Principal Dashboard
* **Schedule Management:** Add, view, and delete class schedules per grade level with conflict detection for teacher time slots.

## VIII. Known Issues and Bug Fixes

The following issues were identified during testing and have been resolved:

### Bug 1: Admission Step 4 (Family Background) Green Checkmark Error

**Issue:** Step 4 (Family Background) in the multi-step admission form always displayed a green checkmark even when no data was entered. This occurred because the `isStepComplete()` function had an empty required fields array for step 4 (`4: []`), and the function returned `true` when the array was empty — treating "no required fields" as "all required fields satisfied."

**Root Cause:** In `resources/views/portal/student/admission-apply.blade.php`, the `isStepComplete(n)` function at line 413 contained:
```javascript
4: [],
// ...
if (fields.length === 0) return true;
```

**Fix:** Changed the logic so that steps with an empty required fields array return `false` instead of `true`. This ensures that a step is only marked complete when its required fields are actually filled in.

**File:** `resources/views/portal/student/admission-apply.blade.php` — `isStepComplete()` function.

---

### Bug 2: Duplicate First-Login Password Change Prompts

**Issue:** Users were presented with two separate password change prompts upon first login: (1) a full-screen blocking modal overlay on the portal dashboard, and (2) a standalone page accessible at `/force-change-password`. Both forms submitted to the same `password.force.update` route, creating confusion and a redundant user experience.

**Root Cause:** The system implemented the first-login password change in two places:
1. A Blade modal partial (`portal/partials/force-change-password-modal.blade.php`) included in the portal layout (`portal/layouts/app.blade.php` at line 481), which renders as a full-screen overlay when `auth()->user()->first_login_at` is `NULL`.
2. A standalone Blade page (`auth/force-change-password.blade.php`) accessible via `GET /force-change-password` route, defined in `routes/auth.php` at line 58.

Both forms used the same PUT endpoint (`password.force.update`), but the standalone page was redundant since the modal already handled the password change inline.

**Fix:** Removed the standalone `force-change-password.blade.php` page and its corresponding GET route. The modal overlay in the portal layout is now the sole mechanism for first-login password changes. The PUT route and controller method were retained for the modal form submission.

**Files Modified:**
- `resources/views/auth/force-change-password.blade.php` — removed (or archived)
- `routes/auth.php` — removed the `GET force-change-password` route (line 58-59)
- `resources/views/portal/layouts/app.blade.php` — unchanged (modal remains at line 481)

---

### Bug 3: No Validation on Step Navigation

**Issue:** Users could navigate between admission form steps without filling in required fields. Clicking the "Next" button would advance to the next step even when mandatory fields on the current step were empty. The step indicator would also incorrectly show steps as completed.

**Root Cause:** The `goTo(n)` function in the Alpine.js `admissionForm()` component used DOM-based validation by querying `[required]` elements within the current step's `data-step` container. However, the validation logic had two problems:
1. It only checked elements with the HTML `required` attribute, but some fields relied on Alpine.js `x-bind:required` (dynamic required) which may not have been evaluated at DOM query time.
2. The visual error highlighting (adding `border-red-500` and `bg-red-50` classes) was applied, but there was no user-facing error message or toast notification explaining what was wrong.

**Fix:** Enhanced the `goTo(n)` function to:
1. Check all input, select, and textarea elements within the current step, regardless of `required` attribute status, to ensure fields are not left empty.
2. Add a visible error message at the top of the form when validation fails, indicating which step has incomplete fields.
3. Scroll the error message into view so the user sees it immediately.

**File:** `resources/views/portal/student/admission-apply.blade.php` — `goTo()` and `submitAll()` functions.

---

### Bug 4: Missing Input Validation on Form Submission

**Issue:** Users could click the "Next" or "Submit Application" buttons without filling in all required textboxes. The system did not prevent navigation or submission when fields were empty, leading to incomplete data being saved or submitted.

**Root Cause:** The form relied on native HTML5 `required` attributes for validation, but Alpine.js-controlled forms can bypass native validation when using `@click` handlers with `this.step = n` assignments. The `goTo()` function checked for empty values but did not provide clear feedback, and the `submitAll()` function only validated fields with the `required` attribute — missing fields that were conditionally required (e.g., SHS Strand when Grade 11 or 12 is selected).

**Fix:** Added comprehensive client-side validation that:
1. Checks all visible input, select, and textarea fields in the current step before allowing navigation.
2. Highlights empty fields with a red border and background.
3. Displays a transient error message ("Please fill in all required fields before proceeding.") at the top of the form.
4. Prevents form submission if any step has incomplete fields, navigating to the first step with errors.
5. Validates conditional fields (e.g., Strand is required only when Grade Level is Grade 11 or 12).

**File:** `resources/views/portal/student/admission-apply.blade.php` — `goTo()`, `next()`, and `submitAll()` functions.

## IX. Requirements Analysis

The developed school information and enrollment management system is composed of public-facing pages, secure authentication, and role-based portals for students, teachers, parents, registrar staff, cashier staff, and administrators. Based on the current implementation, the system now includes expanded controls for admissions verification, tuition and payment handling, academic scheduling, grading, announcements, audit logging, and academic event toggles.

### System-Wide Technical Constraints

The system enforces a global session idle timeout of 30 minutes, after which any inactive user is automatically logged out and must re-authenticate. All passwords must be at least 8 characters long and must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. Institutional emails are restricted to the `@agnusdei.edu.ph` domain only. File uploads across all modules are capped at 5 MB per file and restricted to PDF, JPG, JPEG, and PNG formats. The system uses the Asia/Manila timezone (UTC+8) for all timestamp operations, displays currency in Philippine Peso (₱) with two decimal places, and follows the ISO 8601 date format (YYYY-MM-DD). Only one active session is permitted per user account at any time — a new login automatically invalidates the previous session. The system supports Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Edge 90+, and Safari 14+ as minimum browser versions.

### 1. Promotional Website Module

The Promotional Website Module serves as the school's online information portal and first point of engagement for visitors. It provides 14 pages including the home page (with published announcements and events), vision, mission, identity, educational philosophy, institutional background, academics, program offerings, admissions, requirements and procedures, discounts and privileges, and contact information. The inquiry system collects first name, last name, and personal email (restricted to Gmail, Yahoo, Proton, Outlook, and Hotmail). The system automatically generates an institutional email (`firstname.lastname@agnusdei.edu.ph`) and a random 8-character alphanumeric password, creates a User and Student record with `pre-admission` status, and sends credentials via email. Rate limiting is applied to prevent abuse.

Inquiry submissions are limited to 5 per IP address per hour and 3 per email address per day, with a 1-hour lockout after exceeding the limit. First and last name fields are each capped at 100 characters. The home page displays a maximum of 10 most recent published announcements and 5 upcoming events. Duplicate submissions from the same personal email are rejected to prevent duplicate account creation. Auto-generated passwords are exactly 8 alphanumeric characters and cannot be customized during the inquiry process. The module does not support inquiry tracking, follow-up scheduling, or admin-side inquiry management beyond account creation.

### 2. Authentication Module

The Authentication Module manages secure access to the system through authentication. It supports login, logout, forgot password, and reset password flows. Users authenticate using their institutional email and password. The system tracks last and first login timestamps. Self-registration is disabled since accounts are created exclusively through the inquiry flow for students or admin/directress action for staff. Upon first login, a floating modal blocks all interaction until the user changes their default password. Profile management allows users to edit their first name, last name, contact number, and profile picture. Active/inactive account status prevents login for deactivated accounts.

A maximum of 5 consecutive failed login attempts triggers a 15-minute account lockout, though an Admin can manually unlock it sooner. Password reset requests are limited to 3 per email address per hour, with reset tokens expiring after 60 minutes. The system prevents password reuse by checking against the last 3 passwords. Account deletion is soft-delete only — records are retained for audit trail purposes. Deactivated accounts receive a generic "Invalid credentials" message to avoid disclosing account status. First-login password change is mandatory and cannot be dismissed without completion. The module does not support two-factor authentication, biometric login, social media authentication, or single sign-on (SSO).

Multi-role assignments are not supported — each user holds exactly one role at all times. Role assignments can only be performed by an Admin. Unauthorized access attempts return a 403 Forbidden error via middleware enforcement. Each role has a dedicated dashboard with no cross-role visibility. The welcome banner can be dismissed once per session per device. An Admin cannot demote their own account. The module does not support custom role creation, role hierarchies, permission granularity beyond the 9 predefined roles, or temporary role assignments with expiration dates.

Student promotion supports promote, retain, graduate, and transfer actions with automatic fee carry-over. CSV exports for enrollments, grades, and collections are also accessible from the admin module.

The active school year follows the format YYYY-YYYY, with only one active school year permitted at a time. An Admin cannot deactivate their own account, and school settings are editable by Admin only. ### 13. Academic Grading and Assessment Module

Once grades are submitted, they cannot be edited by the teacher — an Admin override is required for corrections. Teachers can only access grades for their own assigned classes. Batch submission is supported for computed grades but not for individual assessment entry.
